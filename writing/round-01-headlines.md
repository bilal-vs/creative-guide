# Writing practice · Round 1 · Headlines

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Goal:** find the headline style that works for Verdant Soft. Headlines are the words on the image, so they carry the post.

**Rules for every option:**
- At most 8 words, sentence case.
- 1–3 highlight words, shown as **[brackets]**.
- No buzzwords and no numbers that aren't confirmed.
- Every claim traces to `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §2 FACTS.

The user marks each option keep or kill and says why. The winners become the headline rules in `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §7.

## Topic A · How we work: milestone payments (fact F12)
For founders who worry about paying for work they can't see.

| # | Headline | Type |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | You pay at **[milestones]** you can see | Plain benefit |
| A2 | Kickoff. Key phases. Delivery. That’s when **[you pay]**. | Rhythm, factual |
| A3 | Your budget, tied to **[real progress]** | Outcome |
| A4 | Pay as the product **[takes shape]** | Image |
| A5 | How **[payments]** work with us | Plain explainer |

**My pick: A2.** It's specific, it has rhythm, and it states the fact itself, not a promise about it.

## Topic B · Engineering insight: multi-tenant data platforms (fact F39, "contributed to")
For CTOs and technical founders.

| # | Headline | Type |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Multi-tenant ETL starts with **[clean isolation]** | Statement |
| B2 | Isolate tenants **[before you scale]** them | Imperative lesson |
| B3 | The **[first rule]** of multi-tenant data | Curiosity |
| B4 | Shared platform, **[separate data]**. Here’s how. | Promise of a how-to |
| B5 | Where multi-tenant platforms **[break first]** | Problem-led |

**My pick: B2.** It's an opinion a senior engineer would actually say, and it gives the reader the lesson in the headline.

## Topic C · Brand world: "Growth, engineered" (facts F02, F14)
For everyone; this sets the brand's point of view.

| # | Headline | Type |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Built for the **[next stage]** of growth | Aspirational |
| C2 | Software that **[grows]** when you do | Warm |
| C3 | Scale is a **[design decision]** | Opinion |
| C4 | Build once. Grow **[without rebuilding]**. | Rhythm |
| C5 | Engineered for the day **[you double]** | Image |

**My pick: C3.** It's short, it's a point of view, and it's ownable. It also fits the creative platform "Growth, engineered."

## Feedback
**User, 2026-10-06** (answered in plan mode):
- **Default style: problem/curiosity**, the B5 type ("Where multi-tenant platforms [break first]"), at about 60% of headlines.
- **The other styles** (opinion claim C3, imperative B2, rhythm A2) fill the rest.
- **Highlights:** keep one highlight (1–3 words) per headline.

**Codified:** `brand/strategy.md` v2 §5 and `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §7 (decided rules).
- **Variety:** never the same formula three days running, and never the same opening word two days running `[decided · CD]`.
