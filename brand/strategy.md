# Verdant Soft: social strategy (handler's view)

> **Status: v2, 2026-10-06.** The user set the content mix, lanes, calendar, sources, voice, captions and carousel rules in a planning session (`learnings/log.md`, 2026-10-06). Those decisions are binding and are written into `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §2.3, §3, §4, §7, §9 and §11. Calls the user delegated to me are tagged `[decided · CD]`. v1 (2026-10-04) was revised after the audit (`brand/research.md` → "Audit results"; raw evidence in `brand/audit-raw.md`).

It's written the way a social media handler thinks: **past** (what the brand has already said and shown), **present** (where it stands and what's holding it back), **future** (what to post, why, and how it grows).

### What changed from v1 (user, 2026-10-06)
1. **Content mix flipped on purpose: 30% Verdant Soft, 70% external.** External means industry facts, trends, "how AI works" explainers, software concepts and startup advice, under a new pillar, **Tech, Explained** (§6).
   - **Why it's a risk:** v1 said insight must come from our own projects, because generic insight posts got the least engagement (4–11 reactions; §1).
   - **Why we're doing it anyway:** the user chose reach and relevance.
   - **Mitigation:**
     - every external post ends with a **Verdant take**
     - it's written for **founders and decision-makers** (the buyers), not a general audience
     - it runs as one of **five named series**, so the feed has a rhythm, not random tips
   - **Review:** after 8 weeks of live metrics, compare external posts with Verdant posts on buyer-side signals (profile visits, website clicks, inbound). Rebalance within the bounds in §12.
2. **One third carousels**, on a fixed 2-2-3 three-week cycle (7 of every 21 posts). Carousels move from the v2 roadmap into v1 (§6, §12).
3. **New weekly rhythm.** Verdant gets the two strongest LinkedIn weekdays (Tue and Thu); the five external lanes get one day each (§6).
4. **Brand world** becomes calendar moments only (§9). It no longer has a weekly slot.
5. **External facts come from primary sources only,** through a checked bank: weekly trend drops now, daily runs at launch. Sources are shown on the image and in the caption (§8).
6. **Voice and caption rules** for both platforms are settled (§5).

### What changed from v0
1. **Visual world.** It now follows the real brand: a **slate blue → sage teal gradient** (#416D95 → #74AFAD) on off-white and ink, set in Inter. **It is not green.** The v0 idea of "green, organic growing forms" is gone. Growth is now shown through structure and light (§3).
2. **Continuity call.** The website is the brand's best expression, so social moves onto it. The past social templates (royal navy with yellow capitals, AI robots, stock people) are dropped on purpose (§1).
3. **Insight posts** must come from Verdant Soft's own projects. The generic insight posts they've run got the least engagement (§2, §6).
4. **Real-people posts** do best, and the pipeline must never fake people. So the news inbox matters more, and inbox items about people jump the queue (§8).
5. **Proof** now has six quotable entries from five clients, plus ten case studies. Thursday alternates **Client words** and **Project spotlight** (§6, §7).
6. **No self-reported numbers** until the user confirms one set. The numbers on the site and on LinkedIn disagree (§10).
7. **Captions get a reset:** 3–5 hashtags instead of 10–16, no Unicode bold, a hook first instead of emoji (§5).
8. **The questions** at the end are refreshed (§14).

## 2. Present: diagnosis
1. **Sounds like everyone.** Thousands of offshore development shops say "innovative, scalable, seamless". So does Verdant Soft's own site copy and LinkedIn Overview. A buyer scrolling the feed can't tell them apart.
2. **The buyer's real question is trust, not capability.** Founders hiring a remote team worry about:
   - missed deadlines
   - poor communication and time-zone silence
   - low code quality
   - developers disappearing mid-project
   - lock-in and losing ownership of the work
3. **Verdant Soft's proof answers those fears in the clients' own words.** The quotes are all in `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §2 `PUBLIC-PROOF-v2`:
   - "took full ownership" (P02, pending confirmation)
   - "proactive communication"
   - "quickly understood our problem"
   - "sought a clear understanding before starting the work"
   - "consistently delivering milestones on time"
   - "maximizing value while managing costs"
4. **There's an underused differentiator: full cycle under one roof.** UX research → design → build (web, mobile, AI SaaS) → cloud and DevOps → a dedicated team. The site says it ("We're full-cycle"), and each service has a defined process.
5. **The name is a gift. The colour is the brand.** *Verdant* means growing. The ownable visual is the slate-blue → teal gradient and the VS mark, not literal green.
6. **Social is a credibility check.** Buyers find Verdant Soft through online search, Upwork, Fiverr or LinkedIn outreach; the Clutch reviewer came from "Online Search". Then they look at the page. The feed has to make them think: *real team, real work, knows what it's doing, still active.*
7. **The feed doesn't look like the company.** Social and the website use different colours, fonts and moods. A buyer who clicks from a post to the site sees two brands.
8. **Real beats generic.** Posts with real people and real events get about 5× the reactions of generic tips. The automation can't make people posts, so its own content (insight, how we work, proof) has to be specific, first-hand and useful, or it won't earn attention.
9. **The numbers don't agree.** The homepage, a LinkedIn post and Clutch give counts that contradict each other. A careful buyer will notice. Until one set is confirmed, posts state no numbers.
10. **Company-page reach is low.** 10K followers, and a typical post gets about 17 reactions. Steady daily posting helps, and staff reshares help more (§12).

## 5. Voice
**Engineer-to-founder:** confident, plain-spoken, specific, warm. It sounds like a senior engineer you'd trust with your product, not an agency brochure. The site's own line is the north star: **"Let's build it right."**

- **Do:**
  - one idea per post
  - concrete nouns and real tech names (from `FACTS`, or from a checked item in the source bank)
  - short sentences
  - show the process
  - admit trade-offs
  - end with a useful takeaway or a clear, low-pressure CTA (book a meeting, or verdant-soft.com/hire-us)
- **Don't:**
  - buzzwords: innovative, cutting-edge, seamless, leverage, synergy, game-changer, unlock, revolutionise, delve, empower, top-tier, "in today's fast-paced world"
  - hype, or more than one exclamation mark
  - emoji walls
  - invented numbers. A number must be `confirmed` in `FACTS`, or come from a checked, unexpired bank item with its source shown
  - posts that make it all about "us" and give the reader nothing

**From the past feed → to the new voice:**

| Past habit | New rule |
|---|---|
| Emoji as the first character ("🚀 Exciting opportunity!") | The first line is a hook in words. **LinkedIn: no emoji. Instagram: max 2, never first, never as bullets** |
| Unicode "bold" letters (𝗟𝗲𝘁’𝘀) | Plain text only (accessibility and search) |
| 10–16 hashtags, dot-spacer lines | **LinkedIn 3, Instagram 5**, at the end, from the per-lane bank (`STYLE_GUIDE.md` §7 `HASHTAGS-v1`), always including #VerdantSoft |
| Generic claims ("top-tier IT solutions") | One specific thing: a real project, a real decision, a real client sentence, or a sourced fact |
| "We are pleased to announce…" | Lead with what the reader gets |

- **Platform register:**
  - **LinkedIn:** fuller and insight-led. 120–220 words, line breaks, hook before "see more".
  - **Instagram:** shorter and visual-led. 40–100 words, punchy first line.
- **Same visual on both platforms, separate captions.** English only.
- **Pronouns:** "we" for our experience ("in our builds…"), "you" for the advice. Never "I"; it's a company page.
- **Stance on AI and tech:** practical and balanced. What it's good for, where it fails, what it costs to build with. No hype, no doom.
- **Explainer depth:** plain language, with **one real technical term per post, defined in one line** ("RAG means the AI looks things up before it answers").
- **Grow with us:** the same engineer voice, warmer. It's speaking to someone starting out. Never "rockstar" or "ninja".
- **The Verdant take** ends every external post.
  - **Default:** advice ("What we'd tell a founder: …").
  - **"In our builds…"** only when a confirmed FACTS entry backs it (our stack F05–F06, our process steps F04). Never invented client work.
- **Endings:** about 1 post in 3 ends with a **specific** question a founder can answer from experience ("What broke first when you scaled?"). Never "Thoughts?". The rest end on the take or a save prompt.
- **CTAs:**
  - **Direct ("Book a call at verdant-soft.com"):** only on How we work and Proof, so at most 2 a week.
  - **External posts:** the take plus a soft action (save it, follow the series).
- **Headlines:**
  - **Problem/curiosity** leads, about 60% ("Where multi-tenant platforms [break first]").
  - **Other styles** fill the rest: opinion claim, imperative lesson, rhythm, plain fact for By the Numbers.
  - **Variety:** never the same formula two days running `[decided · CD]`.
  - **Highlight:** one per headline (1–3 words).

(Exact caption templates are decided in `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §7 after testing.)

**Format:** one post per day, cross-posted. The same visual goes to both platforms, with **separate captions** per platform.

**Weekly rhythm:**

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **This Week in Tech** (roundup carousel) | wk A: **How we work** · wk B: **Build Notes** | **AI, Explained** | wk A: **Proof** · wk B: **Grow with us** | **Founder Notes** | **By the Numbers** | **Under the Hood** |

- **Week A and week B:** alternate by ISO week number; even weeks are A.

  - Cycle week 2: Mon + Tue
  - Cycle week 3: Mon + Fri + Sun

## 7. Recurring series (memory and continuity)
Series give the feed a rhythm, give followers a reason to come back, and give the automation a clear "what's next". Episodes are numbered and tracked in `posts/index.md`. Every post carries its series name as a small pill on the image.

**Tech, Explained (external, written for founders):**

| Series | Day | Lane | Idea | Format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **This Week in Tech** | Mon | Trends & news | 3–5 checked items from the week's trend drop, each with its source, then our take on the one that matters most to founders | Carousel, every week |
| **AI, Explained** | Wed | AI & LLMs | How it actually works: next-word prediction, context windows, RAG, agents, evals, hallucination, cost per call. One term defined per post | Carousel in cycle week 1, otherwise single |
| **Founder Notes** | Fri | Startup & product advice | Decisions founders face: scoping an MVP, what to build first, working with a dev team, buy vs build | Carousel in cycle week 3, otherwise single |
| **By the Numbers** | Sat | Facts & numbers | One sourced number, what it means, and what a founder should do about it. The source sits on the image | Single |
| **Under the Hood** | Sun | Software concepts | One concept a founder keeps hearing (API, caching, multi-tenancy, CI/CD, tech debt), explained plainly | Carousel in cycle week 3, otherwise single |

**Verdant Soft:**

| Series | Pillar | Idea | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Build Notes** | Build Notes | One real engineering decision per episode, from a case study or the stack (F05–F06, F30–F39): the problem, the choice, the trade-off | Open-ended |
| **Outsourcing, Decoded** | How we work | One buyer fear per episode, answered with real facts: how payments work (F12), what if I don't know what I need, can I hire for one service (F15), changes after launch, time zones, code ownership, handover | ~12 episodes, then refreshed |
| **Wireframe → Production** | How we work | Their real processes, one step per episode: UI/UX six steps (F04), then the custom-software and DevOps steps | Finite (~12–18), can re-run yearly |

## 8. Inputs: how the handler decides what to post today
Priority order. The first one that applies wins.
1. **News inbox:** real things the team supplies, such as a launch, a new hire, a campus drive, a culture day (Mango Day), an expo, a new review, or a milestone.
   - **Optional.** Nothing breaks if it's empty. The format is in `inbox/README.md`.
   - **Real photos** are allowed only from here.
   - **Inbox items take the day,** replacing **that day's external post** (never a Verdant slot). Max one per day; the rest queue.
2. **Calendar moment** for today (§9). It also replaces that day's external post.
3. **Timely trend item** that fits today's lane (`banks/trends.md`, checked and unexpired).
4. **Series episode** due for today's slot.
5. **Evergreen** topic from the lane's topic bank (`banks/topics.md`), respecting no-repeat windows.

**Bumped posts:**
- An evergreen post moves to the next free external slot.
- A trend post expires, unless the Monday roundup can still use it.

**Verdant share:** the band is 25–40% a month. When inbox items would push it over 40%, they queue.

**Research that feeds the banks:**
- **Now:** a scheduled run every Saturday (09:59 PKT) adds 3–5 checked trend items from primary sources. Trend items expire after 14 days.
- **At launch:** a daily run adds 1–3 items, plus major breaking news. The Saturday run then picks the week's best 3–5 for Monday's roundup.
- **Evergreen bank:** refreshed monthly.
- **Rules:** `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §2.3 `EXTERNAL-SOURCES-v1`.

Hiring posts use **only roles supplied through the news inbox.** The careers page's job list was broken during the audit, and the pipeline never invents roles.

## 10. Guardrails (proposed)
- **Facts:** state only facts from the fact bank (`STYLE_GUIDE.md` §2 `FACTS`) marked `confirmed`. Never invent metrics, clients, awards, years or team size.
- **Numbers:** no self-reported numbers (project counts, client counts, satisfaction rates, user reach, savings percentages) until the user confirms them (F50–F52 are pending). Never state team size or headcount.
- **Proof:** quote only `quote_verbatim` or `alt_quotes` text from `PUBLIC-PROOF`, character for character, with the exact `attribution`. Max 1 proof post per week. The same `client_key` not again within 30 days. Never print star ratings.
- **Case studies:** never name the client. Keep the scope words ("built the … modules", "contributed to"); never inflate them ("redefined…"). Never quote the case-study "The Process" boilerplate.
- **Services:** name only the four services in F02. Never "Marketing" or "Business Development", which past graphics listed.
- **People:** never present AI-generated people as Verdant Soft staff, clients or events. Talent and culture visuals are conceptual, or use people who can't be identified (hands, silhouettes, shot from behind, out of focus).
- **No famous-people quotes or likenesses** (e.g. the past Mark Zuckerberg quote card).
- **Culture and religion:** use only the pre-approved templates for national and religious days. Never generate Arabic or Urdu script, religious figures, or sacred sites.
- **Brands:** no third-party logos or trademarks in images. Tech names in captions are fine.
- **Identity:** never confuse the company with other "Verdant" companies.
- **External content (user, 2026-10-06):**
  - **Sources:** primary sources only. Stats no older than 2 years, never quoted second-hand. The source goes on the image and in the caption.
  - **Other companies:** named in text when they're the subject. Neutral and factual; no logos, no verdicts or comparisons, never a claimed partnership.
  - **Trends:** never presented as Verdant experience.

  Full rules: `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §2.3 and §9.
