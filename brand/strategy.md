# Verdant Soft: social strategy (handler's view)

> **Status: PROPOSAL v0, 2026-10-04.** Written before the audit of past posts (see `brand/research.md`, "Audit TODO"). It gets revised after the audit, then the user approves it. Once approved, its rules move into `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §3 and §11. Until then, nothing here is binding on the pipeline.

It's written the way a social media handler thinks: **past** (what the brand has already said and shown), **present** (where it stands and what's holding it back), **future** (what to post, why, and how it grows).

---

## 1. Past: what exists already
- **Website copy:** "innovative software, digital transformation, scalable web and mobile applications". It is accurate but generic. The tagline "Engineering tomorrow's tech today!" is still unconfirmed.
- **Proof:** one Clutch review (all 5.0 scores) and three website testimonials, from WEMASY, Elia Essen and Alogirft(?).
- **Social history:** a LinkedIn company page exists. No Instagram was found. **Past posts aren't audited yet.** That audit decides continuity:
  - what to keep, so followers recognise the brand
  - what to fix, if the look or voice is weak
  - what to drop
- **Rule for the handler:** change the brand on purpose, not by accident. If the audit shows an established look, evolve it. If there isn't one, set it now and hold it.

## 2. Present: diagnosis
1. **Sounds like everyone.** Thousands of offshore development shops say "innovative, scalable, seamless". A buyer scrolling the feed can't tell them apart.
2. **The buyer's real question is trust, not capability.** Founders hiring a remote team worry about:
   - missed deadlines
   - poor communication and time-zone silence
   - low code quality
   - developers disappearing mid-project
   - lock-in and losing ownership of the work
3. **Verdant Soft's proof already answers those fears.** Every public testimonial says the same things: *on time, responsive, receptive to feedback, reliable, dependable, took full ownership.* That's the positioning, and it's backed by evidence, not invented.
4. **There's an underused differentiator: full cycle under one roof.** UX research → design → build (web, mobile, AI SaaS) → cloud and DevOps → a dedicated team. Many small shops only write code.
5. **The name is a gift.** *Verdant* means lush and growing. It gives the brand an ownable visual and verbal world that no competitor can easily copy.
6. **Social is a credibility check.** Buyers often first meet Verdant Soft on Upwork, Fiverr or through LinkedIn outreach, then look at the page. The feed has to make them think: *real team, real work, knows what it's doing, still active.*

## 3. Future: positioning and brand idea
**Positioning (internal):** *Verdant Soft is the engineering partner that owns the outcome: from first wireframe to production cloud.*

**Creative platform: "Growth, engineered."** This is internal, not a public tagline. It's one idea that serves all three goals:

| Goal | What "growth, engineered" means |
|---|---|
| Clients | Products built to scale: architecture, cloud and process that hold up as the business grows |
| Brand | A clear point of view on building software well: practical, specific, opinionated |
| Talent | Careers that grow: real international projects, modern stack, learning |

**Visual world (direction, not final):** engineering precision meets living growth. Structure, systems, architecture and light, joined with green, organic, growing forms. The palette waits for the audit (real brand colours).
**Clichés to avoid:** a plant sprouting from a laptop, lightbulbs, coins and growth arrows, handshakes, robots, glowing brains, generic "hacker in hoodie", stock-style smiling teams.

## 5. Voice
**Engineer-to-founder:** confident, plain-spoken, specific, warm. It sounds like a senior engineer you'd trust with your product, not an agency brochure.

- **Do:**
  - one idea per post
  - concrete nouns and real tech names
  - short sentences
  - show the process
  - admit trade-offs
  - end with a useful takeaway or a clear, low-pressure CTA
- **Don't:**
  - buzzwords: innovative, cutting-edge, seamless, leverage, synergy, game-changer, unlock, revolutionise, delve, "in today's fast-paced world"
  - hype, or more than one exclamation mark
  - emoji walls
  - invented numbers
  - posts that make it all about "us" and give the reader nothing
- **Platform register:**
  - **LinkedIn:** fuller and insight-led. 120–220 words, line breaks, hook before "see more".
  - **Instagram:** shorter and visual-led. 40–100 words, punchy first line.

## 6. Content pillars (draft)
| # | Pillar | Goal | What it covers | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Engineering insight** | Brand / thought leadership | One practical lesson: cloud cost, CI/CD, Kubernetes, React performance, MERN architecture, building AI SaaS, scaling for traffic spikes | ~29% |
| 2 | **How we work** | Clients | Takes the risk out of outsourcing: process, the UX → DevOps pipeline, the dedicated-team model, communication, handover, ownership | ~29% |
| 3 | **Proof** | Clients | Public testimonials and outcomes only. **Max 1 per week; same client not again within 30 days** (user decision: public proof is OK but not too much) | ~14% |
| 4 | **Grow with us** | Talent | Learning, stack, internships, open roles, campus drives. **No AI-generated "employees"** | ~14% |
| 5 | **Brand world / Moments** | Brand | Visual-led "Growth, engineered" pieces, calendar moments, company milestones | ~14% |

**Format for v1: one post per day, cross-posted.** The same image goes to both platforms, with **separate captions** per platform. That halves the generation and judging cost, keeps the brand consistent, and fits the 4:5 format on both. Platform-specific posts are a v2 option.

**Weekly rotation (draft):**

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Insight | How we work | Insight | Proof\* | Grow with us | Brand world | How we work (light) |

\* If no proof item is eligible (because of the caps or because nothing is quotable yet), Thursday falls back to *How we work*.

## 7. Recurring series (memory and continuity)
Series give the feed a rhythm, give followers a reason to come back, and give the automation a clear "what's next". Episodes are numbered and tracked in `posts/index.md`.

| Series | Pillar | Idea | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Build Notes** | Insight | One engineering lesson per episode | Open-ended |
| **Outsourcing, Decoded** | How we work | One buyer fear per episode, answered honestly: time zones, communication, code ownership, quality, handover, scope creep, … | ~12 episodes, then refreshed |
| **Wireframe → Production** | How we work | Walks through their real 7-step UX process, then build and DevOps | Finite (~10), can re-run yearly |
| **Grow at Verdant** | Talent | Skills, stack, intern advice, what the work is like | Open-ended |

## 8. Inputs: how the handler decides what to post today
Priority order. The first one that applies wins.
1. **News inbox:** real things the team supplies, such as a launch, a new hire, a campus drive, a new review, or a milestone. Real photos are allowed only from here. The format will be defined in §11 of the guide, as a folder or sheet the pipeline reads.
2. **Calendar moment** for today (see below).
3. **Series episode** due according to the rotation.
4. **Evergreen** topic from the pillar's topic bank, respecting no-repeat windows.

Hiring posts read open roles from the **careers page**. They never invent roles.

## 10. Guardrails (proposed)
- **Facts:** state only facts from the fact bank (`STYLE_GUIDE.md` §2 `FACTS`). Never invent metrics, clients, awards, years or team size.
- **Proof:** quote only verbatim text from `PUBLIC-PROOF`. Max 1 proof post per week. No client again within 30 days.
- **People:** never present AI-generated people as Verdant Soft staff, clients or events. Talent and culture visuals are conceptual, or use people who can't be identified (hands, silhouettes, shot from behind, out of focus).
- **Culture and religion:** use only the pre-approved templates for national and religious days. Never generate Arabic or Urdu script, religious figures, or sacred sites.
- **Brands:** no third-party logos or trademarks in images. Tech names in captions are fine.
- **Identity:** never confuse the company with other "Verdant" companies.

## 11. Posting time
The buyers are abroad, so optimise for **their** mornings, not Lahore's:
- Central Europe 08:00–10:00 CET = about 12:00–14:00 PKT
- US East 08:00–10:00 ET = about 18:00–20:00 PKT

**Proposal:** LinkedIn at about 13:00 PKT on weekdays, and Instagram at about 19:00 PKT, which also suits local talent in the evening. To be confirmed in the guide.

## 13. KPIs (after launch only; nothing is published during the build)
- **LinkedIn:** follower growth, engagement rate, page and website visits, inbound messages and leads
- **Instagram:** saves, shares, reach, follows
- **Talent:** applications mentioning social, campus-drive turnout
