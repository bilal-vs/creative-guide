# QuoteCard

The Proof post for client words: a verbatim testimonial on a white panel over the Mist ground, in the light theme.

- **You provide:** one quote taken exactly from the proof bank (`quote_verbatim` or an `alt_quotes` entry), and its `attribution`, printed exactly.
- **No generated image.** The quote is the picture. The large quotation mark is a graphic in `brand-gradient`, and a faint mesh line in `motif` sits at the bottom.
- **Length:** quotes up to about 25 words fit at the `quote` size. A longer quote steps down to `subhead` size. Never trim inside a quote.
- **Caps:** at most one proof post a week, and the same client not again within 30 days.
- **Don't:**
  - star ratings
  - edited or merged quotes
  - client logos
  - photos of the client
