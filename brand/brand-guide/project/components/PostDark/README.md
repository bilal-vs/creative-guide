# PostDark

The dark-theme post: Deep Slate ground, near-white headline, a sage-teal or gradient highlight, white lockup top-left.

- **Use for:** Engineering insight and Brand world. About three posts a week.
- **You provide:** the generated image (low-key, lit from within, deep slate negative space behind the text zone), an optional series `label`, one `headline`, an optional `subhead`, and the caption line.
- **Motif:** the white VS mark, oversized and cropped off an edge at about 5% opacity, is optional. Use it on Brand world posts, or when the image is sparse.
- **Do:**
  - highlight in `highlight` (sage teal), or `brand-gradient` text at 24px and up
  - use slate blue for glows, fills and large type
- **Don't:**
  - royal navy or pure black grounds
  - small slate-blue text
  - yellow
  - glowing robot or brain clichés in the image
