# PostLight

The light-theme post: Mist ground, ink headline, one or two words in slate blue, gradient lockup top-left.

- **Use for:** How we work, Proof (project spotlight), Grow with us. About four posts a week.
- **You provide:** the generated image (calm and airy, empty space behind the text zone), an optional series `label`, one `headline` of up to 8 words, an optional `subhead`, and the caption line.
- **Text block:** sits top-left below the logo, or bottom-left above the caption. Never over the image's subject.
- **Do:**
  - highlight 1–3 words in `highlight` (slate blue)
  - keep sage teal to shapes and fills
  - keep everything inside `safe-x` / `safe-y`
- **Don't:**
  - gradient text on light
  - sage-teal text
  - centred headlines
  - all caps
  - more than one headline
